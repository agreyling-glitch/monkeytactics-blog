These posts cover the MonkeyTactics blog as a project: why it exists, what it publishes, and how it complements the main tools website. Expect updates, editorial context, and explanations of the subjects covered here, including privacy-first utilities, word games, calculators, QR codes, browser technology, and practical software design.

The articles aim to answer the questions that do not fit beside a button or input field. Why does one approach protect privacy better than another? What makes a generated result reliable? How can a player use a solver without removing all the challenge? This category provides orientation to that editorial approach and points toward the growing groups of how-to guides, technical deep dives, and decision-focused comparisons.

New readers can begin here, while returning readers can use the topic archives and article links to explore a subject in greater depth.
