Explore how markets shape the tools and technology people use. These articles examine consumer behavior, product demand, pricing, competition, and the incentives behind online services. The focus is practical rather than speculative: understanding why products change, how business models affect users, and what broader trends mean for people choosing digital tools.

Market context is especially useful when a seemingly simple product decision has consequences for privacy, ownership, or long-term cost. A free service may be supported by advertising, lead generation, subscriptions, or hosted features that create ongoing dependence. The guides collected here separate genuine technical requirements from commercial choices and help readers evaluate products based on how they operate, not only how they are promoted.

Read the linked articles for concrete examples, supporting evidence, and questions you can use when comparing competing services or emerging product categories.
