This collection brings together articles about MonkeyTactics itself. Learn why particular tools were built, how they work, what privacy and reliability choices guide their development, and how to use them effectively. It also includes project updates and behind-the-scenes context that does not fit inside a calculator, generator, or solver interface.

MonkeyTactics focuses on utilities that solve a clear problem without demanding unnecessary accounts, uploads, or subscriptions. Different tools have different requirements, so the articles describe privacy claims precisely rather than treating “local” or “private” as vague marketing labels. You will also find explanations of validation, export formats, browser performance, and the testing needed to make a convenient utility dependable in real use.

Use this archive to follow the project across releases and understand how individual features fit into the broader collection of MonkeyTactics utilities.
