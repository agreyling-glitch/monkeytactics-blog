Use this search page to find MonkeyTactics articles by subject, tool, game, or technical concept. Enter a word or short phrase in the search box—for example, `QR code`, `Scrabble strategy`, `password generator`, or `mortgage calculator`. Results update in the browser and link directly to the matching guides.

Search covers the published article titles, summaries, and page content available in the blog index. Begin with the most specific important words and shorten the query if you do not see the result you need. Alternate terminology can also help: try `word finder` instead of `unscrambler`, or `error correction` instead of `ECC`.

The blog focuses on practical online tools, privacy-conscious software, word games, calculators, QR codes, and the engineering decisions behind browser applications. You can also browse the [complete article archive](/posts/) or choose a topic from the tag links shown throughout the site.

Search is powered by a local blog index loaded into your browser. Your query is used to filter that index on the page; it is not submitted to a MonkeyTactics search server as part of the search function. As with any website visit, ordinary hosting, network, and browser behavior still applies. Avoid entering passwords, financial details, personal information, or other sensitive data into any site search field.
